【台南】給外國同事的兩條散步路線

赤崁樓到傳統市場、孔廟到林百貨——附地圖，文末有英文版

Posted by sysherry on Sunday, August 23, 2026

給外國朋友：English version is at the bottom of this post. Jump to English

同事要來台南出差，問我短短的空檔可以去哪裡走走，我就整理了兩條路線給他。兩條都用走的就可以，不用交通工具。

路線一：赤崁樓到傳統市場

想吃在地小吃的話走這條。牛肉湯、米糕都在這一帶。

市場裡那家米糕我記得有被米其林指南收錄，有時間的話一定要吃吃看。

附近的酒吧也很多，晚上想續攤很方便。

路線二：孔廟一帶

台南孔廟是台灣第一所官辦學校，很有歷史。

附近的林百貨很適合買伴手禮。逛完之後推薦去莉莉水果店吃一碗綜合水果冰——芒果季雖然過了，但其他選擇還是很多。

地圖 / Map

English

Two walking routes in Tainan, for colleagues and friends visiting on a short trip. The map is above.

I’d like to recommend two great walking routes for you.

Route 1: Chikan Tower to the traditional market

If you want to try some local food, like beef soup or rice cake (Mi Gao), here is the place to go. I remember the rice cake inside the market was recognized by the Michelin Guide, so definitely give it a try if you have time!

Also there are lots of bars nearby.

Route 2: Around the Confucius Temple

The Confucius Temple was historically the first official school in Taiwan. Nearby, Hayashi Department Store is a great spot to pick up some souvenirs.

After walking around, I recommend to stop by Lily Fruit Shop for a mixed fruit shaved ice. Even though mango season is over, they still have lots of great options!

Enjoy your walk!
